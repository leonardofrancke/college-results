About College Results

What this site is, who runs it, and what it isn't.

In short: College Results is an independent, student-built website where Live Oak students can share the college decisions they received, so later students can see where people applied and what happened. It is not an official school or district website.

What it's for

Applying to college is easier when you can see what happened to students like you. Students choose to share their GPA, optional test scores and other details, and the decisions they got from each college. The site groups those reports into charts and summaries.

The numbers describe what the students who chose to share reported. They are not admission chances, and they don't account for essays, recommendations, activities or anything else colleges consider. How the numbers work explains what each figure means and its limits.

Who runs it

College Results is an independent project built by a student. It is not run by Live Oak High School or Morgan Hill Unified School District, and neither the school nor the district has sponsored, reviewed or approved it. Its statistics are not official school or district statistics.

Contact: leonardo.francke@gmail.com. Use it for questions, corrections, deletion requests or concerns about anything on the site.

How information gets here

  • Students submit their own results with the "Submit yours" form. There is no student login, and the form doesn't ask for a name, email or student ID.
  • Submissions are self-reported and are not checked against school records.
  • Results are shown publicly without names. The privacy policy explains what is shown, what is hidden, and how to delete an entry.

Using the site responsibly

  • Only submit your own results.
  • Don't try to work out who a particular entry belongs to.
  • If you think an entry is fake, wrong, or identifies someone, email the contact above.
