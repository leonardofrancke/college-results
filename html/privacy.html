Privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Short version: we never ask for your name, email, student ID, or any login. What you submit (your stats and college decisions) is shown publicly on this site without your name so other Live Oak students can learn from it. You can delete it any time. We don't sell data, show ads, or share data with anyone.

Who runs this site

College Results is an independent, student-built site for Live Oak students. Questions or requests: leonardo.francke@gmail.com.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, student ID, or address
  • Passwords or accounts. There is no student login.
  • Your IP address. Our server logs record only the time, the page requested, and the response code.

What you can choose to submit

Only if you fill out the "Add your results" form:

  • Graduation year, unweighted and weighted GPA (required)
  • SAT, ACT, class rank, a self-rating of your essays, intended major, and whether you're a first-generation college student (all optional)
  • For each college: its name, the decision, the application round, school type, and whether you're enrolling, got a scholarship, or were a recruited athlete

Class rank is never shown exactly. It's displayed only as a range of seven places (for example "8–14").

What's public

Your stats and decisions are visible to anyone who visits the site, grouped so readers can see which colleges one (unnamed) student applied to. Some things are never shown publicly:

  • Your exact class rank (only the seven-place range)
  • Whether you're a first-generation college student or a recruited athlete (only the site's admins see these)
  • When you submitted
  • Any college fewer than two Live Oak students applied to. Your answers for it are stored, but not shown until a second student adds the same school.

In a single school, a very unusual combination of details could still let someone guess who you are, so leave out any optional field you'd rather keep private.

Questions you send

Questions typed into the "Ask a question" box go only to the site's admins. They aren't linked to your submission or your browser, and they're never shown publicly. Please don't include your name or other personal details.

How your entry is linked to you

Your browser stores a random ID (in local storage, not a cookie) so you can come back and edit or delete your entry. That ID is never shown publicly, and it's the only way to edit your entry. The site also stores your light/dark theme choice and whether you've dismissed a couple of notices. Nothing is used to track you across other sites.

Usage statistics

To see which features people use, we count page views and clicks on colleges and filters. These records use a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of the browser ID, so they can't be tied back to a submission.

Third parties

We don't share, sell, or rent any data, and there's no advertising or third-party analytics. The site doesn't load anything from other companies: fonts and all other files are served from our own server.

How long we keep data

  • Submissions: until you delete them, or until you ask us to
  • Usage statistics: kept, but never linked to a submission
  • Questions: until an admin dismisses them

Deleting your data

On the same browser you submitted from, open "Add your results" and click Delete my submission. It's removed immediately. If you've switched browsers or cleared your data, email us with the colleges and decisions you submitted and we'll find and delete it.

Who can see the raw data

Only the site's administrators, who sign in with a password. They see the submitted fields, including the ones hidden from the public, but never your browser ID. They use that access only to answer questions, remove spam, and honor deletion requests.

Age

This site is meant for high school students applying to college. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
