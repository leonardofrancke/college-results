Terms of Use

Draft prepared October 2026 · Effective date: [not set]

1. What College Results is

College Results ("the site") is an independent, student-built website where students share the college admission decisions they received so other students can learn from them. [Operator: name of the person or organization legally responsible for the site, to be confirmed.]

The site is not an official website of Live Oak High School or Morgan Hill Unified School District. [Replace this paragraph if the district later agrees to a formal relationship, and describe that relationship accurately.]

2. Who can use it

Anyone can view the results. The submission form is meant for Live Oak High School students reporting their own college decisions.

  • [Decision needed: minimum age, and whether a parent or guardian must agree before a student under 18 submits.]
  • [Decision needed: whether submitting is limited to current students and recent graduates, and how that would be checked, if at all.]

3. What you can submit

  • Only your own results. Don't submit information about another student, even with their permission. [Confirm whether a parent or counselor may submit on a student's behalf.]
  • Be accurate. Report the decisions you actually received. Leave out anything you're unsure of.
  • No identifying details. Don't put names, emails, phone numbers, student IDs, social media handles or other identifying information in any field, including college names, majors and questions.
  • Optional means optional. You never have to fill in optional fields. Skipping them is the best way to make an entry harder to connect to you.

What happens to the information you submit, what is shown publicly, and how to delete it are explained in the privacy policy.

4. Using the results appropriately

  • Don't try to figure out who a particular entry belongs to, and don't share guesses about it.
  • Don't copy the site's data in bulk, republish it, or combine it with other information to identify students.
  • Don't use the site to harass, embarrass or pressure anyone about their results.
  • Don't submit fake entries, flood the site with submissions or questions, or try to break, overload or get around how the site works.

5. The numbers are not advice

Results are voluntary and self-reported, are not verified, and often come from very few students. They aren't admission chances, official statistics or counseling advice. Talk to a school counselor before making decisions about where to apply. See How the numbers work.

6. Reporting a problem

If you think an entry is false, was submitted about someone else, could identify a student, or is otherwise inappropriate, email leonardo.francke@gmail.com. Describe the entry (college names and decisions) without including anyone's personal details. [Decision needed: who reviews reports, how quickly, and what can be removed.]

The "Ask a question" box sends a message to the site's administrators. It is not monitored in real time and is not a way to get urgent help. [Confirm wording once a support process is agreed.]

7. Content we may remove

The operator may remove entries or questions that break these terms, appear to identify someone, appear fake, or were reported by the person they describe. [Decision needed: moderation process, notice to the submitter (if any is possible without contact details), and record-keeping.]

8. Availability and changes

The site is provided as a free, volunteer project. It may change, be unavailable at times, or be shut down. [Decision needed: what happens to submissions if the site is shut down or handed over to someone else.]

If these terms change, the updated version and its date will be posted here. [Decision needed: how changes are announced, and how agreement to a new version would be recorded, if at all.]

Questions about these terms: leonardo.francke@gmail.com. [Confirm the long-term contact address and who is responsible for answering it.]
