Accessibility

Last reviewed October 16, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use College Results, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or other assistive technology. The site does not yet fully meet the standard below, and this page says where it falls short.

Our target

We're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. We don't claim the site conforms to it.

What has been checked

In October 2026 the results page, its dialogs, the question box and these information pages were checked in Google Chrome on a Mac, using made-up test data:

  • Using a keyboard only: skip link, navigation, school filters, chart (college names and dots), Students-like-me tabs, college details, Compare, and the statistics notice.
  • That dialogs have a name, move focus in, keep focus inside, close with Escape where allowed, and return focus to where you were.
  • Layout at 320, 390 and 768 pixels wide and on a desktop screen, which matches browser zoom of 200% and 400% on a typical laptop. These were simulated window sizes, not real phones.
  • Colour contrast in light and dark themes, reduced-motion settings, and an automated scan (axe-core) for common WCAG 2.1 A and AA issues.

Not yet checked: screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS), real phones and tablets, school Chromebooks, Safari and Firefox, and Windows High Contrast mode.

Known problems

  • Submission form. The "Submit yours" form hasn't been updated yet. Some of its fields have no programmatic label, the college-name suggestions can't be chosen with the arrow keys, the decision menus get cramped on small phones, and errors appear as pop-up alerts. Changes to the form are on hold while the way submissions work is reviewed.
  • The chart. The scatter chart is keyboard-operable, but it is dense. "Show chart as a table" under the chart lists the same colleges and decision counts in a table.
  • Language. The site is in English only.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site doesn't work for you, or you need information in a different format, email leonardo.francke@gmail.com. Say which page and what you were trying to do. Please don't include personal or student information.
