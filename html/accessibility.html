Accessibility

Last reviewed November 25, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use College Results, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or other assistive technology. The site does not yet fully meet the standard below, and this page says where it falls short.

Our target

We're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. We don't claim the site conforms to it.

What has been checked

In October 2026, and again after the November 2026 redesign, the results page, its dialogs, the question box and these information pages were checked in Google Chrome on a Mac, using made-up test data:

  • Using a keyboard only: skip link, navigation, school filters, chart (college names and decision marks), the "You" slider, Students like me, the Colleges, Numbers and Students views, sorting and filtering students, saved colleges, college details, Compare, and the statistics notice.
  • That dialogs have a name, move focus in, keep focus inside, close with Escape where allowed, and return focus to where you were.
  • Layout in simulated browser windows 320, 390, 768 and 1280 pixels wide. Narrow windows of 640 and 320 pixels were used as an approximation of a laptop screen at 200% and 400% zoom. Actual browser zoom was not tested, and neither were real phones.
  • Colour contrast in light and dark themes, reduced-motion settings, and an automated scan (axe-core) for common WCAG 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA issues. The submission form is now part of the automated scan.

Not yet checked: actual 200% and 400% browser zoom, screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS), real phones and tablets, school Chromebooks, Safari and Firefox, and Windows High Contrast mode.

Known problems

  • Submission form. Every field now has a label, but the college-name and major suggestions can't be chosen with the arrow keys, and errors appear as pop-up alerts.
  • The chart. The chart is keyboard-operable, but it has many marks. "Show chart as a table" under the chart lists the same colleges and decision counts in a table.
  • Language. The site is in English only.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site doesn't work for you, or you need information in a different format, email leonardo.francke@gmail.com. Say which page and what you were trying to do. Please don't include personal or student information.
