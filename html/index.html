A quick note on these numbers

A GPA band with a lower acceptance rate usually just means those students applied to harder schools, not that a higher GPA worked against them.

Some groups here are only one or two students, and this page can't see essays, recommendations, or anything else that actually decides an application.

Don't let one data point talk you out of applying somewhere you're excited about.
